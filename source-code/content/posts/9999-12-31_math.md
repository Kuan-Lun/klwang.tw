## 題目

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over a field $F$, and let $W$ be a subspace of $V$. Show that there exists a linear transformation $T: V \mapsto V$ whose null space and range are both $W$ if and only if $\dim V = 2 \dim W$.

## 答題

Denote the nullity of $f$ by $\operatorname{nullity}(f)$, and the rank of a linear function $f$ as $\operatorname{rank}(f)$.

Suppose first that there exists a linear transformation $T: V \mapsto V$ whose null space and range are both $W$. Then
$$
\operatorname{nullity}(T)=\dim W
\quad\text{and}\quad
\operatorname{rank}(T)=\dim W.
$$
By the dimension theorem (Friedberg et al. (2002), Theorem 2.3),
$$
\dim V
=\operatorname{nullity}(T)+\operatorname{rank}(T)
=2\dim W.
$$
This proves the necessity.

Denote the null space of a linear function $f$ by $\mathrm{N}(f)$, the range of $f$ by $\mathrm{R}(f)$. The notation $V=W\oplus U$ means that every $v \in V$ can be written uniquely as $v=w+u$, where $w \in W$ and $u \in U$.

Conversely, suppose that
\[
\dim V=2\dim W.
\]
Extend a basis of \(W\) to a basis of \(V\), and let \(U\) be the subspace spanned by the additional basis vectors. Every \(v\in V\) can then be written uniquely as
\[
v=w+u,
\qquad w\in W,\quad u\in U.
\]
This is expressed by
\[
V=W\oplus U,
\]
where \(\oplus\) denotes the direct sum. Moreover,
\[
\dim U=\dim V-\dim W=\dim W.
\]
Hence, there exists an isomorphism \(S:U\to W\).

Define \(T:V\to V\) by
\[
T(w+u)=S(u).
\]
The uniqueness of the decomposition \(v=w+u\) ensures that \(T\) is well-defined, and the linearity of \(S\) implies that \(T\) is linear. Since \(S\) maps \(U\) onto \(W\),
\[
\mathrm{R}(T)=S(U)=W.
\]
Moreover, because \(S\) is injective,
\[
T(w+u)=0
\iff S(u)=0
\iff u=0.
\]
It follows that
\[
\mathrm{N}(T)=W.
\]
Thus, there exists a linear transformation \(T:V\to V\) whose null space and range are both \(W\). This proves the sufficiency and completes the proof.

## Reference

Friedberg, S. H., Insel, A. J., and Spence, L. E. (2002). Linear Algebra. *Pearson*, Upper Saddle River, 4th edition.
